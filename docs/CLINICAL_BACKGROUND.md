# Clinical Assessment and Medical Documentation

## Original Research - 2023

The electronic stethoscope was developed for the acquisition, transmission, recording and processing of cardiac sounds.

The original research included an exploratory clinical evaluation at Hospital de Clinicas, La Paz, Bolivia.

Five cardiac sound recordings were acquired and evaluated by medical specialists.

## Medical Diagnostic Certificates

The researcher maintains signed medical diagnostic certificates supporting the clinical findings documented during the study.

These certificates constitute medical documentation associated with the original research.

Clinical diagnoses were established by qualified medical professionals.

The device itself did not automatically generate diagnoses.

The signed certificates are retained privately and are not published with patient-identifying information.

## Hospital Authorization

The researcher confirms possession of signed hospital authorization expressly permitting publication of the study's cardiac sound recordings.

The original signed documentation remains part of the research records.

Two WAV recordings are available in the public repository as research audio samples.

No patient names or identifying clinical histories are attached to the recordings.

## Engineering Evaluation

The research documented the operation of the following components:

- Conventional stethoscope acoustic coupling.
- MAX9814 microphone amplification.
- ESP32-based acquisition.
- Wi-Fi transmission.
- Python audio reception.
- WAV recording and playback.
- Digital filtering.
- Spectral visualization.
- Custom mechanical enclosure.

The prototype was used to acquire cardiac acoustic information for subsequent medical evaluation.

## Interpretation

The medical certificates document clinical findings assessed by professionals.

The technical evaluation documents the prototype's signal acquisition and processing capabilities.

These are complementary forms of evidence.

The exploratory assessment was not designed as a statistical diagnostic-accuracy study.

Sensitivity, specificity and regulatory certification are not claimed.

## Research Continuation

The 2026 modular digital stethoscope is a separate hardware development.

Its proposed architecture includes reversible acoustic coupling, low-noise acquisition and an ESP32-S3 platform.

Its performance must be evaluated independently of the original prototype.
