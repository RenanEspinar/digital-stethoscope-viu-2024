## Terminology

- **Gallop rhythm:** an auscultatory finding (typically S3 and/or S4), not a disease named “gallop syndrome.”
- **Irregular rhythm:** an acoustic observation that can raise suspicion of arrhythmia; classification or confirmation of a cardiac arrhythmia generally requires ECG documentation.
- **Clinician-reviewed:** a cardiologist interpreting recordings; distinct from an AI system automatically classifying disease.
