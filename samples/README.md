# Cardiac Sound Recordings

## Research Context

This directory contains two cardiac acoustic WAV recordings associated with the original electronic stethoscope research conducted at Hospital de Clinicas, La Paz, Bolivia.

The recordings were obtained using the experimental electronic stethoscope system.

## Publication Authorization

The researcher confirms that signed hospital documentation expressly permits publication of the research sound recordings.

The signed authorization is retained in the original research records.

Signed medical diagnostic certificates also form part of the study documentation.

These records are not published with patient-identifying information.

## Medical Evaluation

The research recordings were evaluated by medical specialists.

Clinical findings were documented by medical professionals.

The electronic stethoscope performed sound acquisition and signal processing, not automated diagnosis.

## Scope of Publication

The audio files are provided as research examples for acoustic inspection and engineering analysis.

Patient names, individual medical certificates and identifying clinical histories are not published.

Public access to these samples does not grant unrestricted third-party redistribution rights.
